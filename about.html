A family plumbing company built on showing up.

Since 2008 we've grown from one van to a team of 14, without losing the personal service that got us started.

Our story

Started by a plumber who was tired of no-shows.

Founder Marcus Bell spent ten years working for large contractors before going out on his own. He kept hearing the same complaint from customers: plumbers who never called back, never arrived on time, or quoted one price and charged another.

So he built ClearFlow around the opposite. Every customer gets a confirmed arrival window, a written price before work starts and a plumber who explains what went wrong and how to stop it happening again.

Eighteen years later, more than half our work comes from repeat customers and their referrals.
