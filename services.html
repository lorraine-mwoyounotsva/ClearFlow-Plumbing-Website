Leak detection & repair

A small hidden leak can waste thousands of gallons a year and quietly damage floors and walls. We pinpoint it with acoustic sensors and thermal cameras, then repair it with as little disruption as possible.

  • Slab and wall leaks
  • Water meter checks
  • Burst pipe repair
  • Ceiling stain tracing
  • Outdoor line leaks
  • Insurance reports
